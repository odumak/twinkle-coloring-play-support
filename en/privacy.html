Privacy Policy

The app does not require an account or ask for names, dates of birth, location, photos, voice recordings or advertising identifiers to send to a developer server. It has no ads, third-party analytics or user tracking.

In-progress coloring and previews, finished PNG pictures, the album index and save dates, and sound and difficulty settings are stored on the device. Parents can delete individual or all album pictures in the Parent Area. Deleting the app removes its data from that device; copies in device backups depend on Apple backup settings.

Only when a parent requests it, the app adds a selected picture to Photos. The device may request permission to add photos. A copy saved in Photos can remain there after deletion from the album or the app.

Apple handles optional in-app purchases and restores. The app checks Apple-verified purchase entitlements on the device and does not send transactions to a developer server. The developer does not receive card details or Apple account passwords.

If a parent sends an email, the operator may see the sender’s address and message to reply. Please do not send children’s names, photos, contact details or other personal information. Request deletion of inquiry records through the email below. The email provider and this website’s hosting service have their own privacy policies.

The parent check restricts access to purchases and external links; it is not a substitute for legally required consent to collect children’s personal data. We will update this notice if practices change.
