Need help?

Choose a picture, color and tool, then rub with a finger or Apple Pencil. Finished pictures can be viewed in the in-app album.

In the Parent Area, change difficulty and sound, delete album pictures, or save a selected picture to Photos. If saving fails, check the device’s permission to add photos and available storage.

To restore extra pictures, choose Restore Purchases in the Parent Area and use the same Apple account. Purchases and restores may require an internet connection and Apple authentication.

When contacting us, include your device, iOS version, app version and the problem. Please do not send a child’s name, photo or other personal information.
